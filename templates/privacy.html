AkuOS Privacy

Privacy Policy

AkuOS is built to help you understand your finances. This page summarizes what data is used and how it is handled.

Data Collected

  • Account information you add or connect, such as account names, balances, and account types.
  • Transaction data, including dates, merchants, amounts, categories, notes, rules, and import history.
  • Settings and preferences needed to run your AkuOS account.

Plaid Connections

When you connect a bank, Plaid handles bank credential collection. AkuOS does not store your bank username or password.

Data Use

Your data is used to power app features like dashboards, transactions, budgets, rules, merchant memory, imports, and account sync.

No Selling Data

AkuOS does not sell your financial data, transaction history, account details, or personal information.

Deletion Requests

You can request deletion of your AkuOS financial data at any time. Some operational records may be retained if required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Data Retention

Data is stored only as long as necessary to provide the service. Unused data may be periodically removed. Read the formal Data Retention and Deletion Policy.
