AkuOS Privacy

Data Retention and Deletion Policy

This policy explains how AkuOS retains, removes, and deletes customer financial data in a controlled, compliance-oriented way.

Necessary retention only Deletion requests Plaid credentials not stored

Purpose and Scope

This policy applies to AkuOS account data, financial records, connected account metadata, transactions, rules, merchant memory, budgets, goals, subscriptions, imports, preferences, logs, backups, and related operational records.

Retention Principle

AkuOS stores customer data only as long as necessary to provide the service, operate the app, support security needs, meet compliance-oriented obligations, or resolve user requests.

User Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of their AkuOS financial data at any time by contacting {{ contact_email }}. Requests are reviewed and processed in a reasonable timeframe.

Plaid Credentials

AkuOS does not store Plaid bank login credentials. Bank credential collection is handled by Plaid during the connection flow.

Inactive Data

Data associated with inactive accounts, stale imports, disconnected integrations, or unused operational records may be periodically reviewed and removed when no longer necessary.

Secure Deletion Procedures

  • User-scoped financial records are deleted from active application storage when a deletion request is completed.
  • Connected account tokens and integration records are removed or invalidated where applicable.
  • Generated imports, previews, and operational artifacts are removed when they are no longer needed.
  • Backups and provider-managed retained copies expire according to applicable infrastructure retention cycles.

Compliance-Oriented Handling

AkuOS aims to keep data retention limited, documented, and purpose-based. Retention and deletion practices may be adjusted to support legal, regulatory, fraud prevention, audit, or security obligations.

Deletion Verification

Deletion work should be performed through controlled application or administrative procedures and reviewed for completion where practical.

Contact

Retention questions or deletion requests can be sent to {{ contact_email }}.

This policy complements the AkuOS Privacy Policy and may be updated as data stores, hosting infrastructure, integrations, or compliance requirements evolve.
