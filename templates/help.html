How AkuOS Works

A quick map of the main workspace and how each part fits together.

Dashboard

Your command center for cash flow, safe-to-spend, upcoming payments, recent activity, and month-level patterns.

Transactions

Search, filter, review, and bulk-edit activity. This is where cleanup work turns raw bank data into useful financial history.

Accounts

Track connected and manual accounts, balances, liabilities, and account-level transaction history.

Import Center

Upload files, review parsed transactions, catch duplicates, and approve imports before they enter your ledger.

Merchant Memory

Teach AkuOS simple merchant preferences, like “when this merchant appears again, use this category.”

Rules

Create stronger matching logic for recurring patterns, renames, tags, skipped transactions, and category automation.
